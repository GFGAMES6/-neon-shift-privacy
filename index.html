Neon Shift does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Third-party services used by the app may process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information directly to us, please contact us so that we can address the issue.

GF does not currently operate its own server-based user account or personal-data database for Neon Shift. Data collected by third-party services may be retained according to those providers' respective policies.
